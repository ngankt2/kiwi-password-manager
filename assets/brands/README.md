# Service and bank logos

These bundled 64 × 64 PNGs identify services and banks in the icon picker, lists and item details. They load from the application bundle and never contact the service.

Sources:

- [Dashboard Icons](https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons), revision `cf87d9bbd47792b20893f090ff48763fe874b05d`. The collection is distributed under the Apache License 2.0; see [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), revision `b86d5c9a0bdd4f3f5c30898a63654dd32f39fd76`, for Bank of America, Barclays, Chase, Deutsche Bank and HSBC. The collection is distributed under CC0 1.0; see [LICENSE-simple-icons.md](LICENSE-simple-icons.md).

Product names and logos remain the property of their respective owners.

Modifications: cropped transparent margins, resized proportionally and centered on a transparent 64 × 64 canvas. Dashboard Icons retain their original colors. Simple Icons SVGs were rasterized using each brand's primary color from the collection's `data/simple-icons.json`.
